A little about me

Design is more than how it looks.

I'm Ummay Salma, a Product Designer and UI & UX Designer with a background in Graphic Design. I enjoy exploring how people interact with digital products and shaping those insights into clear, useful interfaces.

My approach brings together visual craft, structured thinking and empathy. I care about the details—from information hierarchy and accessibility to the small interactions that make a product feel effortless.

✦ Curious by nature
⌁ Detail-oriented
◎ User-focused
↗ Always learning
